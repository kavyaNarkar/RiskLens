Privacy Policy

Effective: February 18, 2026 • Updated: February 18, 2026

1. Introduction

RiskLens provides website security assessment and compliance scoring services. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, storage, and protection of information when you interact with our platform. By utilizing RiskLens, you formally agree to the terms prescribed in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We are committed to data minimization and collect only the technical information strictly necessary to deliver our security services effectively.

a) User-Submitted Information

We collect the specific website URLs that users voluntarily submit for the purpose of vulnerability scanning and security assessment.

b) Automatically Generated Data

During the course of our operations, our system automatically generates and logs the following data points:

  • Comprehensive security scan results and analysis
  • Calculated risk scores indicating security posture
  • Compliance scores relevant to industry standards
  • Detailed information on detected vulnerabilities
  • Generated PDF reports available for user download
  • Timestamps of scan activities to maintain a history log

We explicitly do not collect the following:

  • Personal browsing history or behavior
  • User passwords or credential data
  • Sensitive personal identification information
  • Payment information, unless specifically required for premium services

3. How We Use Information

The data we collect is utilized to facilitate and improve our security services in the following ways:

  • To perform rigorous security analysis and vulnerability detection
  • To generate accurate compliance and risk assessment scores
  • To produce detailed, downloadable PDF reports for user reference
  • To maintain a comprehensive history of past scans for your review
  • To continuously enhance the accuracy, performance, and reliability of our platform

We maintain a strict policy of not selling or renting user data to any third parties.

4. Data Storage

We implement robust storage protocols to ensure data availability and security:

  • Scan results are securely stored on our local servers to provide you with access to your scan history.
  • Generated PDF reports are temporarily retained to facilitate convenient downloading.
  • We employ industry-standard technical safeguards to protect all stored data against unauthorized access.
  • Users are strongly advised to scan only public-facing systems and to avoid submitting URLs containing confidential internal systems unless properly authorized.

5. Legal Compliance

RiskLens operates in full compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Irrespective of jurisdiction, we adhere to core data protection principles including:

  • Lawful and transparent processing of data
  • Minimization of data collection to what is strictly necessary
  • Limitation of data use to specified, legitimate purposes
  • Implementation of robust security safeguards

6. Third-Party Services

To deliver our services effectively, RiskLens may utilize trusted third-party resources such as:

  • Reliable hosting providers
  • Secure backend development frameworks
  • Advanced PDF generation libraries

We strictly do not share your vulnerability scan data with third-party marketing services or unauthorized entities.

7. User Responsibilities

As a user of our platform, you bear certain responsibilities:

  • You must only scan websites that you own or for which you have explicit authorization to assess.
  • You must not utilize the platform for any malicious activities, unlawful purposes, or unauthorized surveillance.

8. Data Retention

Our data retention policies are designed to respect user privacy:

  • We retain scan data only for the duration necessary to provide our services and to improve platform functionality.
  • Users retain the right to request the permanent deletion of their stored scan history at any time.

9. Security Disclaimer

RiskLens provides automated risk assessments based on known vulnerabilities. Please be aware that these results are indicative and do not guarantee complete security or absolute compliance with all regulatory standards. They should be used as part of a broader security strategy.

10. Updates to Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect the evolution of our platform features, such as new monitoring capabilities or user account systems. Your continued use of the platform constitutes your acceptance of these updated terms.
